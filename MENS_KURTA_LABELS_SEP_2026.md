# Lesser-known Indian men’s and unisex kurta labels under ₹2,000

**Research date:** 19 September 2026  
**Scope:** Independent or relatively niche Indian labels/stores with their own HTTPS storefront and online cart/checkout. Large marketplaces, Manyavar, Tasva, House of Pataudi, Fabindia, and obvious national chains are excluded.

## How to read this list

- **Bands** are selling-price bands in which at least one kurta was listed or indexed: **A** = ₹0–999, **B** = ₹1,000–1,499, **C** = ₹1,500–1,999.
- `Sale` means the label appears to qualify only, or most clearly, at a discounted price. Prices, sizes, coupons, and stock can change quickly.
- A stable **collection URL** is used where it was corroborated; otherwise the link is the retailer’s own HTTPS product-search URL rather than a fragile individual SKU.
- **High confidence** means exact price evidence or same-day band corroboration was captured. **Medium** means the direct storefront was corroborated but an exact current SKU price could not be retained. **Medium–Low** is a useful Google/index-listed lead whose price and checkout should be rechecked before relying on it.
- Direct site fetching was partly blocked by retailer anti-bot responses, so this is deliberately not presented as a guarantee of live inventory.

It is corroborating indexed evidence rather than a guarantee of a live September SKU.
4. Exact Kraft India evidence came from this public [product-grid snapshot](https://github.com/jdurdundi-ux/frontend-/blob/master/src/Components/Body.jsx), including Kraft India’s own CDN image and the ₹1,599 product text.
5. The lower-confidence entries are retained because the brief allows Google-listed stores and prioritizes breadth among smaller labels. They are intentionally marked **Medium–Low**, not promoted as live-verified bargains.

## Checkout checklist

Before paying, confirm that the selected item is a **kurta** rather than only a photographed set component, that the final selling price remains below ₹2,000, and that the required size is available. Also check delivery postcode, shipping charge, return eligibility, fabric composition and whether a coupon—not the displayed price—is required to enter the stated band.
